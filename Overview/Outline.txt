Repo is a centralized code based for the TTRPG game made in Unreal, aiming to be a steam game

Inspiration
    Improvement on web based TTRPG services such as Roll 20 and DND Beyond.  Both are clunky and limited in their tool set, causing issues for both players and DMs
    Players need easier access to their sheet for rolls, ideally customizable sheets that could be modified by DM or by player.  Roll 20 is closer to good for this, but restricted to DND, so it should be more open.  Players need individual and centralized tool systems and support for player inventory, a trouble in all TTRPG
    DMs need a very wide access of tools that are restricted on both ends.  They need ways to see what players see, quickly and easily move players, and ways to make scripts or marcos to speed up the process

        Easy to transfer data from different games
